Upgrade Sequence (Earned Nirvana)
---------------------------------

You do **not** need to jump to the full A-grade system all at once. Treat this as a staircase:

1. **Step 1 – Heavy Iron First (Biggest Jump):**  
   Upgrade only the output transformers from GXSE to CXSE while keeping the rest of the B-grade amp intact. This immediately tightens bass, adds weight, and improves treble decay without changing your familiar circuit.
2. **Step 2 – Speakers (Room as an Instrument):**  
   Move from the Klipsch/bookshelf tier to a 95dB+ full-range or large-cone design (Zu/Tekton/Lii). This unlocks effortless dynamics and lets the CXSE iron really breathe in your room.
3. **Step 3 – Source & Phono (Micro-detail):**  
   Upgrade to a heavier, better-isolated turntable and a dedicated outboard phono stage. Here you gain soundstage depth, image stability, and the “air” above cymbals and strings.
4. **Step 4 – Power Supply Speed & Bypass Caps:**  
   Swap in the Mundorf/Solen film caps in the first supply node and upgrade the cathode bypass caps (Elna Silmic II / Kaisei). This step removes grain and veil, making transients snappier and backgrounds blacker.
5. **Step 5 – NOS Glass & Silver Wire (Fine Brushstrokes):**  
   Only after you know the sound of your modern tubes and copper wiring should you drop in the NOS Amperex/Mullard glass and silver signal wiring. This is where the last 5–10% of holography, tone color, and “you are there” realism appear.

Assembly Guide: A-Grade Nuances
-------------------------------

1. **Chassis Space:** The Edcor CXSE transformers are drastically larger than the GXSEs. Ensure your 14"x8" walnut chassis has the physical real estate on the top plate to hold ~10 lbs of iron without bowing. (You may need thicker 3/8" top plating).
2. **Silver Soldering:** Cardas Quad Eutectic solder melts at a slightly different temperature than standard lead/rosin. Use a high-quality, temperature-controlled soldering iron and ensure the silver wire is perfectly clean before making joints.
3. **Film Cap Mounting:** Polypropylene motor-run caps are huge (often the size of a D-cell battery). You will need to plan your internal chassis layout carefully to mount the Mundorf TubeCap using a chassis clamp.
4. **Cathode Resistor Heat:** With the heavy iron, your tubes will be running at optimal Class-A dissipation. Ensure your 270Ω cathode resistors are rated for at least 5W (preferably 10W wirewound) and elevated off the wood base to dissipate heat.
